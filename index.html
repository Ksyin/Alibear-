Many devices are simple to set up yourself. For locks, cameras and full systems our local team can install for you. Ask us before ordering.

Tell us about your doors, Wi-Fi and what you already own, and we will confirm compatibility before you buy.

How do I pay?

Create an account at checkout and pay securely with Paystack. Ask our team about other payment options.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes, we deliver across Kenya. Contact us for timing and costs to your area.
